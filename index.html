The flow

Three steps, no clinic visit required to get started

Answer a few symptoms

Cough duration, fever, night sweats, weight loss — the same red‑flag markers a nurse would ask about.

2

Why it works

An acoustic AI model, plus a transparent symptom screen

Acoustic ensemble

A 300‑tree random forest trained on 13 MFCCs plus spectral centroid, zero‑crossing rate and RMS energy from real TB and healthy cough recordings. It runs entirely in your browser.

Guideline symptom screen

Your answers are checked against the WHO four‑symptom screen — cough, fever, night sweats, weight loss — plus blood in cough and HIV status. It is rule‑based and visible, so a reassuring cough sound never hides a worrying symptom.

Built to refer, not diagnose

Every Medium or High result comes with a clear next step: get a GeneXpert or sputum test at your nearest clinic.

About us

A student‑built screening tool with a real clinical brief

Cough Afrika started as MediCode&Cure: CoughAfrika, a final‑year CSIT capstone project focused on a hard, practical question — can the sound of a cough alone help flag tuberculosis risk early, in places where lab testing is slow or far away?

We built and evaluated the model against real linked data: verified TB and healthy cough recordings paired with matching clinical symptom records, benchmarking several models before settling on the acoustic model that powers the screening flow today, alongside a guideline‑based symptom screen.

The team spans machine learning, full‑stack development, and clinical‑risk research — the same mix of skills the judging brief called for: data understanding, modelling, and documentation that holds up to scrutiny.

FAQ

Common questions

Is this a TB diagnosis?+

No. Cough Afrika is a triage and screening aid. A Medium or High result means "go get a confirmatory test" — GeneXpert MTB/RIF or sputum microscopy — not "you have TB."

Where does my cough recording go?+

Your recording never leaves your device: the AI model runs inside your browser and the audio is analysed in memory and discarded. Accounts and your history stay in your browser. Only if you tick the optional consent box when saving a result, we also send your name, age, scores and triage result (never the audio) to MediCode&Cure's secure database so a clinician can follow up.

Who is this built for?+

Both community members who want a quick first check, and health workers who need a fast triage tool for high‑volume screening in resource‑limited settings.

Does it work without a data connection?+

The screening flow itself runs entirely in your browser once the page has loaded, so it works offline after the first load. The planned WhatsApp version depends on your normal data or Wi‑Fi.

What does a Medium or High result mean I should do?+

Visit your nearest clinic for a sputum or GeneXpert test — sooner for High risk, within a few days for Medium — especially if your cough, fever or night sweats persist. A Low result does not rule TB out — if symptoms continue, get tested anyway.
